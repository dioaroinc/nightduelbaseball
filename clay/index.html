SKY SHOT
CLAY
Clay shooting: 6 rounds of 15 targets, 90 clays in all. Trap, skeet and high driven birds, then a fast mixed round to finish.
  1. One thumb does it all. Press and hold the FIRE button (or anywhere on the screen): that calls PULL! and the clay flies.
  2. Keep holding and slide your thumb to swing the gun onto the clay. The button fills as you mount the gun, and the ring around your sight grows with it: a full ring is your widest shot.
  3. Let go to fire. Shot takes a moment to get there, so on a crossing clay aim a little ahead of it. Hold too long and the gun gets heavy: the ring shrinks again.
