SWISH SHOT
BASKETBALL
60 seconds on the clock. Shoot from five spots around the key and score as many points as you can before the buzzer.
  1. Press and hold anywhere. The power fills up. Let go at the top, while it says SWISH. Too early is short, too late is long.
  2. Slide left or right while you hold to keep the target on the rim. Your hand drifts a little, so keep it centred.
  3. A basket is 2, a clean swish is 3. Hit 3 in a row and you are on fire: +1 on every basket until you miss.
