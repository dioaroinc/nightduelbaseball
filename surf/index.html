CREST RIDER
SURF
Waves roll in from the left. Paddle into one, pop up right as it breaks, and ride it as far toward the beach as you can.
  1. Hold anywhere to paddle. Start early: it takes a moment to get up to speed.
  2. Let go right as the wave breaks (the top starts to spray, then curls over). Too early and it rolls under you. Too late and you go over the falls.
  3. Every wave is a different height. Bigger waves go further, but the timing is tighter. A perfect catch on a big one takes you all the way to the beach.
  4. 5 waves. Your score is the total distance. Your longest ride is saved too.
