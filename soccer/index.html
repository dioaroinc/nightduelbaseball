SPOT KICK
SHOWDOWN
A penalty shootout. You and your rival take turns: you shoot, then you keep goal. 5 kicks each, and if it is level after that, sudden death.
  1. Your kick: press and hold anywhere. The power fills; slide your thumb to move the target in the goal. Let go to shoot. Let go while it says FULL: hold too long and the ball flies high.
  2. In goal: when the kicker starts the run-up, hold anywhere and slide the glove to where you think the ball is going. Let go to dive. Go too early and the kicker may read you, go too late and you won’t get there.
  3. Corners are hard to save but easy to miss. Aim inside the posts.

YOUR PLAYER

LEVEL

Easy: a slow keeper, softer shots coming at you, and the kicker gives the side away. Pro: a sharp keeper and hard, low-margin shooting.
