TENNIS
One set against the CPU with real tennis scoring: 15, 30, 40, deuce, first to six games by two, tiebreak at 6-6.
  1. You are the yellow circle. Move it with your left thumb (touch anywhere on the left half and slide). The yellow mark shows where the ball will bounce.
  2. With your right thumb, hold anywhere on the right half: the power gauge rises once and falls back over one second.
  3. Let go to swing as the ball reaches your circle (the ring closes on the ball). Middle of the circle and the top of the gauge hit hardest. Slide right or left before letting go to aim; early swings pull cross-court, late ones push wide.
  4. To serve: hold the right side to toss, let go at the top.
