BOWLING
Ten-pin bowling: 10 frames, two balls a frame, three in the 10th if you earn it. Real scoring, so strikes and spares carry over. A perfect game is 300.
  1. Slide the stick at bottom left (or anywhere on the left half) to move where you stand.
  2. Set your spin with the curved arrows: the ball goes straight at first, then hooks that way near the pins. Aim for the pocket, just right of the head pin (or just left if you hook right).
  3. Hold the THROW button with your right thumb (or anywhere on the right half). It fills up and then drops. Let go at the top for full speed and a clean release.
